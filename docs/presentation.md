## Key Takeaways

1. Copilot is a **co-worker, not a replacement** — use it to amplify your work.
2. **Prompting is a skill** — the more specific and contextual your prompts, the better the results.
3. **Iterate** — treat Copilot's first response as a draft, not a final answer.
4. **Governance matters** — Copilot respects permissions; good data hygiene leads to better and safer results.
5. **Experiment** — try Copilot in the apps you use most and build your own prompt library.
