# Project Atlas — Project Brief
**Owner:** April Dunnam · **Sponsor:** VP Operations · **Status:** Kickoff

## Objective
Migrate Northwind Traders' regional order-processing from three legacy systems to a single cloud platform, cutting order-to-ship time by 30% by end of Q3.

## Scope
- In: order intake, inventory sync, shipping handoff, reporting.
- Out: finance/GL integration (phase 2), retail POS.

## Timeline (high level)
- Weeks 1–2: Discovery & requirements
- Weeks 3–6: Build & configure
- Weeks 7–8: UAT & training
- Week 9: Go-live

## Known risks
1. Legacy data quality is uneven (duplicate SKUs).
2. Warehouse team availability during peak season.
3. Two vendor contracts renew mid-project (see Contracts folder).
