# Northwind Traders — Brand & Document Standards

Use this as the "standard" for audit-against-a-standard exercises.

## File naming
- Format: `YYYY-MM-DD_Project_DocType_vN` (e.g., `2026-07-01_Atlas_StatusReport_v2`)
- No spaces; use underscores. Kebab-case for skills.

## Voice & tone
- Plain, direct, active voice. Short sentences.
- Lead with the outcome, then the detail.
- Avoid jargon and internal acronyms in customer-facing docs.

## Formatting
- Titles in sentence case.
- Dates as "1 Jul 2026" in prose; ISO (2026-07-01) in file names/data.
- Currency as USD with a $ symbol and thousands separators.

## Status labels (only these)
- On Track · At Risk · Blocked · Complete · Not Started
