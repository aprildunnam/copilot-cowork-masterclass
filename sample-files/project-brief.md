## Project Brief: Employee Onboarding Tracker

**Project Name:** Employee Onboarding Tracker (EOT)  
**Requested By:** HR Department  
**Date:** July 2026  
**Priority:** High

---

### Background

The HR team currently manages new employee onboarding using a combination of email, shared spreadsheets, and paper checklists. This process is manual, inconsistent, and difficult to monitor at scale. As the company has grown from 150 to 400 employees over the past two years, the existing approach is no longer sustainable.

### Problem

- No centralized system to track onboarding progress across departments
- HR managers spend ~5 hours per week following up on incomplete onboarding tasks
- New employees frequently report confusion about what to do during their first two weeks
- Compliance tasks (e.g., policy sign-offs, IT provisioning) are sometimes missed

### Proposed Solution

Build an internal **Employee Onboarding Tracker** tool — a lightweight web application integrated with Microsoft 365 — that:

- Provides a structured checklist for each new hire
- Sends automated reminders to employees and managers
- Gives HR a real-time dashboard of onboarding progress
- Tracks compliance milestones and flags incomplete items
