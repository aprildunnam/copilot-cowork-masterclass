# Sample: Quarterly Sales Data

Use this sample data in **Challenge 4 — Data Story**. Enter these values into a new Excel spreadsheet to practice using Copilot for data analysis.

---

## How to Set Up the Spreadsheet

1. Open a new Excel workbook.
2. Create the following table starting at cell **A1**.
3. Use **Format as Table** to make it a proper Excel table (Copilot works best with formatted tables).

---

## Data Table

| Region | Q1 Sales ($) | Q2 Sales ($) | Q3 Sales ($) | Q4 Sales ($) | Total ($) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| North | 125,400 | 138,200 | 142,800 | 157,300 | 563,700 |
| South | 98,700 | 95,100 | 101,400 | 110,200 | 405,400 |
| East | 210,500 | 198,300 | 225,100 | 241,800 | 875,700 |
| West | 87,200 | 91,600 | 88,400 | 79,300 | 346,500 |
| Central | 143,600 | 149,100 | 155,700 | 161,400 | 609,800 |

---

## Suggested Analysis Questions for Copilot

Once your table is set up in Excel, try asking Copilot:

- *"Which region had the highest total sales?"*
- *"Which region showed the most consistent growth quarter over quarter?"*
- *"Create a bar chart comparing total sales by region."*
- *"Which region had the lowest Q4 performance?"*
- *"Summarize the sales performance data in 2-3 sentences."*

---

## Notes

- All values are in USD.
- Data represents fictional FY2025 quarterly sales figures for illustration purposes only.
