## Email Thread: Project Kickoff Follow-Up

---

**From:** Sarah Mitchell <s.mitchell@contoso.com>  
**To:** Jordan Lee <j.lee@fabrikam.com>  
**Date:** Monday, 14 July 2026, 09:14  
**Subject:** RE: Project Kickoff — Onboarding Tracker

Hi Jordan,

Thanks so much for the demo on Friday — the team was really impressed. The real-time tracking dashboard in particular got a lot of positive feedback.

Looking forward to getting started!

Best,  
Sarah

**From:** Jordan Lee <j.lee@fabrikam.com>

## Suggested Copilot Prompts for This Thread

**In Outlook (using Copilot to summarize):**
- *"Summarize this email thread."*
- *"What are the open action items from this thread?"*

**To draft a reply:**
```
Draft a reply from Jordan to Sarah's latest email. Address each of her three points: 
confirm the pricing breakdown will be sent by Tuesday, say the vendor credentials 
contact is Alex Torres (a.torres@fabrikam.com), and confirm the data architecture 
doc will be uploaded to the SharePoint by Wednesday. Keep the tone friendly and professional.
```
