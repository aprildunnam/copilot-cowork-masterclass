## How to Use These Challenges

1. Open the challenge file and read the scenario.
2. Follow the **prompt suggestions** or create your own.
3. Evaluate the output using the **success criteria** in each challenge.
4. Iterate and improve your prompts based on the results.
5. Share what you learned with the group!

---

## Tips for All Challenges

- 🎯 **Be specific** — vague prompts produce vague results.
- 🔄 **Iterate** — your first prompt is rarely your best. Refine it!
- 🧠 **Add context** — tell Copilot who you are, what you need, and why.
- 📐 **Specify format** — ask for bullet points, tables, paragraphs, etc.
- ✅ **Review the output** — Copilot can make mistakes; always check the result.
