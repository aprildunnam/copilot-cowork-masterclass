# Challenge 1 — Email Makeover

## 🎯 Scenario

You need to send a follow-up email to a client after a project kickoff meeting. You've jotted down rough notes but need a polished, professional email.

**Your raw notes:**
> met with contoso yesterday. they liked the demo. need to send them the timeline and pricing doc. also remind them to send us access credentials by friday. sarah is the main contact. keep it friendly but professional

---

## 💡 Your Mission

Use **Copilot in Outlook** to turn those rough notes into a polished email.

---

## Suggested Prompts

### Starter Prompt
```
Draft a follow-up email to Sarah at Contoso after our project kickoff meeting yesterday. 
Include: confirmation that they liked the demo, that we will send over the timeline and 
pricing document, and a reminder for them to send us their access credentials by Friday. 
Tone: friendly but professional.
```

### Refinement Prompts (try these after the first response)
- *"Make the email shorter and more concise."*
- *"Add a subject line and make the opening more engaging."*
- *"Rewrite the closing paragraph to include a call to action."*

---

## ✅ Success Criteria

Your final email should:
- [ ] Have a clear, relevant subject line
- [ ] Open with a friendly acknowledgment of the meeting
- [ ] Mention the timeline and pricing document
- [ ] Include the Friday deadline for credentials
- [ ] Close with a clear next step
- [ ] Be professional and free of jargon
- [ ] Be appropriately concise (no more than ~150 words)

---

## 🔑 Key Learning

Copilot works best when you give it structure — even rough notes can be transformed into polished communication when you provide context, tone, and key points in your prompt.
