# Challenge 3 — Document from Scratch

## 🎯 Scenario

Your manager has asked you to create a **project proposal document** for a new internal tool that will help the HR team track employee onboarding progress. You need to produce a professional first draft quickly.

---

## 💡 Your Mission

Use **Copilot in Microsoft Word** to draft a complete project proposal document from a short description.

---

## Suggested Prompts

### Starter Prompt
```
Draft a project proposal document for an internal employee onboarding tracker tool 
for the HR department. Include the following sections: Executive Summary, Problem 
Statement, Proposed Solution, Key Features, Timeline, Estimated Budget, and Next Steps. 
Tone: professional and persuasive.
```

### Refinement Prompts (try these after reviewing the first draft)
- *"Rewrite the Executive Summary to be more concise — no more than 3 sentences."*
- *"Add a section called 'Success Metrics' after the Proposed Solution section."*
- *"Make the Timeline section more specific by suggesting a phased approach with three phases."*
- *"Change the tone of the Next Steps section to be more action-oriented."*

### Bonus Prompt
```
Summarize this document in 5 bullet points suitable for a leadership briefing.
```

---

## ✅ Success Criteria

Your final document should:
- [ ] Include all requested sections
- [ ] Have a clear, professional Executive Summary
- [ ] Describe the problem and solution clearly
- [ ] List at least 4 specific key features
- [ ] Include a realistic, phased timeline
- [ ] Be free of obvious errors and inconsistencies
- [ ] Flow logically from section to section

---

## 🔑 Key Learning

Copilot in Word can generate a structured first draft in seconds. Your job shifts from writing to **editing and refining** — a much faster workflow. Iteration with targeted follow-up prompts is the key to a high-quality result.
