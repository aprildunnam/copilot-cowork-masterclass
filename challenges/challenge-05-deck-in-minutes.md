# Challenge 5 — Deck in Minutes

## 🎯 Scenario

You've been asked to present a 10-minute overview on **"How AI is Transforming the Modern Workplace"** to your team next week. You need a polished PowerPoint presentation — fast.

---

## 💡 Your Mission

Use **Copilot in Microsoft PowerPoint** to generate a complete presentation from a prompt.

---

## Suggested Prompts

### Starter Prompt (Create the Deck)
```
Create a 10-slide PowerPoint presentation titled "How AI is Transforming the Modern 
Workplace." Include an introduction slide, slides covering key areas of AI impact 
(productivity, communication, data analysis, creativity), a section on responsible AI, 
and a closing slide with key takeaways and a call to action. Use a professional tone 
and include speaker notes for each slide.
```

### Refinement Prompts (after reviewing the initial deck)
- *"Make slide 3 more visual — replace the bullet points with 3 icons and short labels."*
- *"Rewrite the speaker notes for slide 5 to be more conversational — I'll be speaking to a non-technical audience."*
- *"Add a statistics slide between slide 2 and slide 3 with 3 impactful AI adoption statistics."*
- *"Change the closing slide's call to action to focus on exploring Microsoft Copilot."*

### Bonus: Create from a Word Document
If you completed Challenge 3, try this:
```
Create a PowerPoint presentation based on the project proposal document. Include a 
title slide, one slide per major section, and speaker notes for each slide.
```

---

## ✅ Success Criteria

Your final presentation should:
- [ ] Have a clear title slide
- [ ] Be 8–12 slides in total
- [ ] Cover at least 3 distinct topic areas
- [ ] Include speaker notes on every slide
- [ ] Have a closing slide with key takeaways
- [ ] Be polished enough to present without major edits

---

## 🔑 Key Learning

Copilot in PowerPoint can generate a full deck from a single prompt — turning the blank slide problem into an editing problem. The more context and structure you provide in your prompt, the more useful the initial output will be.
