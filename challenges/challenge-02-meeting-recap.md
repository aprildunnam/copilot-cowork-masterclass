# Challenge 2 — Meeting Recap

## 🎯 Scenario

You just finished a 45-minute project planning meeting in Teams. Five people attended and discussed project milestones, risks, and next steps. You need a structured recap with action items to share with the team.

---

## 💡 Your Mission

Use **Copilot in Microsoft Teams** to generate a meeting recap and action item list after a meeting.

> **Note:** To complete this challenge with real data, start a Teams meeting (even a short one), speak or type some discussion points, then use Copilot's "Recap" feature after the meeting ends.

---

## Suggested Prompts

### In Teams Meeting (during the call)
```
Summarize what has been discussed so far.
```
```
What are the key decisions made in this meeting?
```
```
List any open questions or blockers mentioned.
```

### After the Meeting (in the Recap tab or Teams Chat)
```
Create a meeting summary with the following sections: Key Discussion Points, 
Decisions Made, Action Items (with owner and due date), and Open Questions.
```

### Refinement Prompts
- *"Format the action items as a table with columns: Task, Owner, Due Date."*
- *"Rewrite the summary so it's suitable to send as an email to stakeholders who weren't in the meeting."*

---

## ✅ Success Criteria

Your meeting recap should:
- [ ] Include a brief overview of the meeting purpose
- [ ] List at least 3 key discussion points or decisions
- [ ] Identify action items with owners and due dates
- [ ] Be formatted clearly and easy to scan
- [ ] Be written at a level appropriate to share with stakeholders

---

## 🔑 Key Learning

Copilot in Teams can automatically transcribe and summarize meetings — dramatically reducing the time spent on note-taking and ensuring no action items are missed. The key is knowing how to prompt for the format you need.
